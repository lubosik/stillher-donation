Why It Matters

This is bigger
than teeth.

Leah Spriggs trusted a doctor referred to her by her church. She went to Johns Hopkins. Thirteen teeth were extracted. When she returned, the doctor had disappeared. No follow-up. No implants. No answers. Left toothless, indefinitely, her body then developed an autoimmune disease.

She returned to the church. They gave her a $25 grocery gift card. Days later, from the pulpit: a $7.2 million renovation announcement. This documentary names what happened. It demands accountability. And it begins the long road to Leah getting her smile back.

She was not asking for luxury. She was asking for her dignity.
