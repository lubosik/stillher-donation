Why It Matters

This is bigger
than teeth.

Lia Spriggs trusted a doctor referred to her through a community she believed in. She went to Johns Hopkins. Thirteen teeth were extracted. When she returned, the doctor had disappeared. No follow-up. No implants. No answers. Left toothless, indefinitely, her body then developed an autoimmune disease.

She went back to that same community and asked for help. They gave her a $25 grocery gift card. Days later, from that same organization, came a $7.2 million renovation announcement. This documentary names what happened. It demands accountability. And it begins the long road to Lia getting her smile back.

She was not asking for luxury. She was asking for her dignity.
